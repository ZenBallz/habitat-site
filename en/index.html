Challenge

Someone buying an apartment will research it more carefully than almost anything they have ever bought. Most of that research happens online, long before the building even exists. Usually, all they find is the same three renders on rotation.

We show them what those renders can’t: the neighbourhood, the materials, the people building it, and tell it as a story worth living in. Published consistently from launch to handover.

Frequently asked questions

Working together

Our broker already promotes the project. What would HABITAT add?

We give your development a dedicated communications programme: a clear identity, original content and consistent presentation across its own channels.

We work with your broker and sales team, using their knowledge of buyer questions to shape content they can also share in follow-ups and before viewings.

Can you work with our existing team, strategy and brand identity?

Yes. We review what is already in place and build on it. An existing strategy becomes a practical content and channel plan, while your brand identity guides the visual presentation.

We can work alongside your marketing team or provide senior communications leadership where there is no dedicated team in-house.

How do content approvals work?

We prepare content for review through a shared approval process. Your nominated contact checks project facts and consolidates feedback from your team.

We agree approval deadlines at the start so content can be published on schedule. The initial rollout includes one consolidated revision round, and its timing depends on timely access to materials, accounts and approvals.

Who answers comments and follows up with potential buyers?

We handle routine social media comments and messages using agreed project information. Questions about availability, pricing, viewings or negotiations go to your sales team.

Sensitive issues and complaints are escalated to the appropriate contact.

Content

What if the development is still under construction?

There is already a story to tell: the architect’s thinking, the layouts, material choices, views, neighbourhood and people behind the project.

We combine approved renders with original photography, interviews and progress updates, clearly distinguishing planned features from completed work.

Who creates the photography, video and graphics?

We organise production and create the visual assets needed for the agreed content plan, using your brand identity and approved project materials.

Essential includes a shoot every two months; Signature includes a monthly shoot. We agree the subjects and required outputs in advance. Additional production can be arranged separately.

Who controls the accounts, and how can we use the content?

Your project’s accounts remain under your control.

Photography includes non-exclusive, perpetual usage rights for your owned digital channels, corporate communications, PR and business-development materials. We confirm any additional permissions needed for advertising or third-party use before publication.

What role does AI play?

AI can assist with research, drafting and production. Effective property communications still require accurate project knowledge, original material, editorial judgement and a clear visual direction.

Our team remains responsible for the content, its accuracy and its presentation.

Results

Can you generate qualified leads?

Paid campaigns can support lead generation where the website, enquiry process and tracking are ready. Objectives and KPIs are agreed according to the media budget, website and available tracking.

Before launch, we agree what counts as a relevant enquiry and how it will be measured. Feedback from your sales team helps us assess lead quality and refine the campaigns.

How will we know whether the work is effective?

We establish a baseline and report monthly on relevant measures, including audience reach, engagement and website clicks.

Where tracking allows, we also report on enquiries and campaign costs. Your sales team’s feedback helps connect the digital data with buyer interest and the quality of conversations.

When should we expect results?

The initial rollout establishes a consistent presentation and regular publishing. We use the first three months to assess audience response and refine content and promotion.

Enquiries and sales also depend on the property, pricing, market conditions, media budget and sales follow-up. We agree realistic objectives for the project’s stage.
