2 Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc., USA. Visiting the website involves processing technical connection and request data, particularly your IP address and, depending on the request, the time, requested file, browser information and referring page. GitHub confirms that it logs visitors’ IP addresses on GitHub Pages sites for security purposes.

We use hosting to deliver and protect the website. The legal basis for processing initiated by us is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in operating a secure, functioning website. GitHub also processes certain operational and security data under its own responsibility. Retention depends on the relevant operational or security purpose, necessary investigations and legal obligations. Further information is available in GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

3 Contact form and email enquiries

When you contact us, we process the information you provide, particularly your name, email address or telephone number and, where provided, your company, project details, preferred contact time and subsequent correspondence. We use this information to respond to your enquiry, arrange a conversation and, where appropriate, prepare a proposal.

For enquiries about entering into a contract with you, processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. For general enquiries, or where you contact us on behalf of a company, we rely on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in handling incoming business enquiries and communicating with business contacts. Spam and abuse prevention also relies on our legitimate interest in keeping the contact channel secure.

Providing information is voluntary. Fields marked as required are needed to identify and respond to your enquiry; without suitable contact details, we cannot reply. Other information is optional. Please do not submit sensitive personal data. Contacting us does not subscribe you to a newsletter or general marketing communications.

4 Formspree

We use Formspree, Inc., USA, to process contact-form submissions. When you submit the form, your entries are transmitted to Formspree, processed and forwarded to us. Technical data, such as your IP address and submission time, is also processed for delivery and abuse prevention. Formspree processes submission data for us as a processor; its own information also applies to certain independent operational and security purposes.

Recipients are the people handling your enquiry at HABITAT and the necessary form, email and IT service providers. Our email providers process correspondence for delivery and storage. Formspree uses infrastructure in the USA. See section 7 and Formspree’s Privacy Policy for further information, including international transfers.

5 Adobe Fonts

With your prior consent, we load fonts through Adobe Fonts, provided by Adobe Systems Software Ireland Limited, Ireland, with involvement of Adobe Inc., USA. Adobe receives your IP address and technical information about the fonts delivered and the requesting website. Adobe uses this information for delivery, troubleshooting and licensing calculations. Adobe states that its webfont service does not use cookies for font delivery and does not store the IP address received for that purpose.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. Without consent, we display fallback fonts. You can change your choice or withdraw consent at any time through . We store your font preference in your browser’s local storage for six months to respect your choice. This preference storage relies on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR and, where applicable, the necessary-storage exception in Section 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021.

Further information: Adobe Fonts privacy information.

6 Cookies, analytics and external links

We do not currently use Google Analytics or other services to analyse visitor behaviour or track visitors for advertising. Technical processing associated with hosting, the contact form and any fonts you enable is described in this policy. Your font preference is stored solely to respect your choice.

If we introduce analytics or marketing services, we will explain the specific processing before introducing them and obtain prior consent where required. This policy does not constitute consent to future services.

Links to our social-media profiles are ordinary links. Following a link takes you to the relevant provider’s service, where its privacy information applies.

7 Transfers outside the EEA

The services described above may involve processing personal data in the USA. GitHub and Adobe state that they use the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for covered transfers and Standard Contractual Clauses where required. Formspree states that, as a processor, it relies on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. Adequacy decisions fall under Article 45 GDPR; Standard Contractual Clauses are safeguards under Article 46 GDPR.

You may request information about the applicable safeguards and a copy of relevant contractual safeguards by contacting anna@habitat-marketing.com. Further information is available from GitHub, Formspree and Adobe.

8 Retention

We delete ordinary enquiries that do not lead to a contract within six months after the enquiry is concluded or the last substantive contact. We delete copies of submissions held in Formspree within 30 days; forwarded emails follow the correspondence-retention period above.

If a contractual relationship follows, we retain the necessary data to perform the contract. Tax-relevant records are generally retained for seven years to meet legal obligations under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR. Where particular information is necessary to establish, exercise or defend specific legal claims, we retain it for the necessary period under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Technical logs and backups follow the relevant providers’ purpose-specific retention and deletion procedures.

9 Your rights

Subject to the GDPR’s conditions, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. Where processing relies on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object on grounds relating to your particular situation. You may withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise your rights, contact anna@habitat-marketing.com. You may complain to a data-protection supervisory authority, particularly in the country of your habitual residence, workplace or the alleged infringement. In Austria: Austrian Data Protection Authority, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, email: dsb@dsb.gv.at, www.dsb.gv.at.

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you.
